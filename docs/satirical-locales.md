# Satirical interface variants

Choose these variants in the language menu. They are opt-in and are not included in random language selection.

- **CorpSlop**: fast-food branding, subscriptions and corporate advertising.
- **English (Israeli PR)**: Israeli government and military press-office rhetoric, self-exonerating statements and media punditry. The jokes target institutions, policies and public statements.
- **限界オタク**: Japanese otaku, gacha and chuunibyou comedy.
- **中文·反审查**: Chinese-language satire of censorship, official propaganda and political authority. The Taiwan-language line is explicitly framed as a joke, not a linguistic classification.

Labels can be irreverent, but tooltips and warnings must still explain the real operation, supported formats and failure conditions. Keep interpolation placeholders unchanged. New languages must preserve the English locale’s key structure and pass the desktop layout check across every theme.

## Quotation sources

The two short Ben Shapiro excerpts in `ui/lang/en_il.json` are attributed in the interface. Text following each attribution is an original joke, not part of the quotation.

- `buttons.preview_tooltip`: his trademark slogan, documented in [his official Daily Wire biography](https://www.dailywire.com/author/ben-shapiro).
- `processing_status`: his remark to Andrew Neil in the [BBC News interview published May 10, 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6VixqvOcK8E), checked against the [interview caption transcript](https://videodb.org/ben-shapiro-us-commentator-clashes-with-bbcs-andrew-neil-bbc-news/6VixqvOcK8E). Punctuation and capitalization are normalized for display.

Other messages are original satire. These variants do not imply endorsement by the people or institutions mentioned.
